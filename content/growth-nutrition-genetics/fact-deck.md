# One-look numbers and cutoffs

## Charts
- WHO 0–5; IAP 5–18
- Source: D13 > Charts

## Short stature
- <−2 SDS
- Source: D13 > Height

## MPH
- Boy +13 cm; girl −13 cm; divide by 2
- Source: D13 > Formula

## Height velocity
- Δheight ×12/months
- Source: D13 > Formula

## Preterm correction
- PNA − (40−GA); PMA = GA+PNA
- Source: D13 > Ages

## ASD
- M-CHAT-R/F 16–30 mo; AAP screens 18/24 mo
- Source: D13 > ASD

## SAM
- WHZ <−3 / MUAC <11.5 cm / edema
- Source: D14 > SAM

## MAM
- WHZ −3 to <−2 / MUAC 11.5–<12.5; no edema
- Source: D14 > MAM

## F-75 / F-100
- 0.75 / 1.00 kcal/mL
- Source: D14 > Feeds

## BF
- First hour; exclusive 6 mo; continue ≥2 y
- Source: D14 > BF

## CF
- Start 6 mo; 2–3 meals 6–8 mo; 3–4 at 9–23 mo
- Source: D14 > CF

## Vitamin A
- 9 mo 100,000 IU; later 200,000 IU, 9 doses total
- Source: D14 > NHM

## Zinc diarrhea
- 2–<6 mo 10 mg; ≥6 mo 20 mg ×14 d
- Source: D14 > NHM

## Iron IDA
- ~3 mg/kg/day elemental common anchor; treatment ≠ AMB
- Source: D14 > Iron

## AR risk
- 25% affected per pregnancy carrier×carrier
- Source: D15 > Pedigree

## Hyperammonemia
- ≥500 µmol/L severe in CHOP; urgent clearance planning
- Source: D15 > Emergency

## OTC vs CPS1
- High vs low/normal orotic acid
- Source: D15 > UCD / Notebook D10 Q65

# Algorithms — ultra-condensed

## Growth chart — sequence
1. Verify age/chart
2. Plot serial values
3. Calculate velocity/MPH
4. Check red flags
5. Investigate or monitor
- Source: D13 > Growth

## Developmental regression — sequence
1. Confirm loss
2. Assess hearing/vision and acute illness
3. Neurologic/metabolic evaluation
4. Start early support
- Source: D13 > Development

## Complicated SAM — sequence
1. Identify any SAM criterion
2. Check appetite/danger signs
3. Facility stabilize with F-75
4. Transition after stabilization
5. Rehabilitation and relapse prevention
- Source: D14 > SAM

## IEM crisis — sequence
1. ABCs and sepsis parallel
2. Prevent catabolism
3. Glucose/gas/ammonia/ketones/acylcarnitines
4. Metabolic consultation
5. Scavengers/clearance if indicated
6. Confirm later
- Source: D15 > IEM

# First-line vs definitive / close-distractor mini-bank

## Weight-first vs height-first
- Decisive clue: Nutrition/systemic vs endocrine/skeletal

## FSS vs constitutional delay
- Decisive clue: BA ≈ CA vs BA delayed

## ASD vs isolated language
- Decisive clue: Joint attention/repetitive patterns vs relatively intact reciprocity

## Screen vs diagnosis
- Decisive clue: M-CHAT and NBS are screens, not definitive tests

## SAM vs severe stunting
- Decisive clue: WHZ/MUAC/edema vs HAZ

## F-75 vs F-100
- Decisive clue: Stabilization vs catch-up

## Vitamin A prophylaxis vs therapy
- Decisive clue: NHM schedule vs urgent xerophthalmia protocol

## CMA vs karyotype
- Decisive clue: CNV vs balanced/aneuploidy

## PWS vs Angelman
- Decisive clue: Paternal expression lost vs maternal UBE3A lost

## UCD vs organic acidemia
- Decisive clue: Resp alkalosis vs ketotic high-AG acidosis

## OTC vs CPS1/NAGS
- Decisive clue: Urine orotic acid high vs low/normal

## FAOD vs hyperinsulinism
- Decisive clue: FFA high vs suppressed with low ketones

# Calculation formula box (worked + independently checked)

## MPH boy — formula?
- MPH boy = (father+mother+13)/2
- Worked example: (174+160+13)/2 → 173.5 cm; do not omit +13

## Height velocity — formula?
- Height velocity = Δheight×12/months
- Worked example: (112−108)×12/8 → 6 cm/year; use cm and months

## BMI — formula?
- BMI = kg / m²
- Worked example: 20/(1.10×1.10) → 16.53 kg/m²; interpret by age/sex

## Corrected age — formula?
- Corrected age = PNA−(40−GA)
- Worked example: 16−(40−27) → 3 weeks; PMA = 43 weeks

## Elemental iron — formula?
- Elemental iron = mg/kg/day × kg
- Worked example: 3×12 → 36 mg/day elemental; salt ≠ elemental

## F-75 energy math — formula?
- F-75 energy math = volume×0.75
- Worked example: 800 mL×0.75 → 600 kcal; NOT a SAM starting prescription

## Carrier×carrier AR risk — formula?
- Carrier×carrier AR risk
- Worked example: 0.25×100 → 25% affected per pregnancy

## Anion gap — formula?
- Anion gap = Na − (Cl + HCO3)
- Worked example: 140−(103+10) → 27 mEq/L; check lab reference and albumin
