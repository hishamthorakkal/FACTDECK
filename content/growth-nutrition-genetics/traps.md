# Growth, Development, Nutrition, Genetics & IEM — System Trap Note

## Trap 1 — AMBER — OTC vs CPS1/NAGS
- Tempting: Low citrulline alone proves CPS1 deficiency.
- Why it looks right: Both proximal UCDs and OTC can have low citrulline.
- Decisive clue: Urinary orotic acid HIGH in OTC, low/normal in CPS1/NAGS.
- Correct rule: Interpret citrulline + orotic acid together.
- Latest protocol: Biochemical UCD pathway; PCRRT 2020 emergency framework
- In the exam: Choose OTC if low citrulline + high orotic acid.
- Source: Notebook > Day10 Q65 (R1 passed 12 Oct) / Day15 > UCD

## Trap 2 — AMBER — FAOD vs hyperinsulinism
- Tempting: All hypoketotic hypoglycemia is FAOD.
- Why it looks right: Both suppress ketogenesis.
- Decisive clue: FFA often high in FAOD but suppressed in hyperinsulinism.
- Correct rule: Use critical-sample ketones + FFA + acylcarnitines.
- Latest protocol: Metabolic diagnostic framework; Day15 IEM
- In the exam: Look at FFA before deciding.
- Source: Notebook > Day10 Q50 (cross-day; R1 passed) / Day15 > IEM
